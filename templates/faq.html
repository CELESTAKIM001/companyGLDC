Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about land, planning, design, development and consultancy.

What services does GLDC provide?

GLDC supports land, planning, design, development and related consultancy workflows. Use the services section or request a quote for a project-specific response.

Can I request a consultation?

Yes. Submit a consultation request with your preferred date and project information and GLDC can follow up.

Can I submit project documents?

Project documents can be handled through the secure operational document workflow. Do not email sensitive documents unless GLDC has instructed you to do so.

How are quotations handled?

Quotations are created, versioned, issued and tracked by authorized GLDC staff. Acceptance and changes remain part of the quotation history.

How are payments verified?

Gateway payments are verified from provider callbacks. Manual payments require authorized staff and a transaction/reference record.

Can GLDC manage ongoing projects?

Yes. The management platform links clients to projects, staff, tasks, milestones, documents, invoices and payment records.
